### Post 4 — Bise vs. Calm Cheat Sheet (Use `winter-ridge.jpg`)
> Why was Gäbris sunny yesterday and grey today? One word: **Bise**. 💨
>
> • **Calm high pressure:** Fog settles around 800–1,000 m.
> • **Weak Bise (NE):** Moisture banks against the pre-Alps → 900–1,200 m.
> • **Strong Bise (NE):** Damming (*Stau*) pushes fog up to 1,400–1,700+ m → head straight for **Kronberg (1,663 m)** or **Hoher Kasten (1,794 m)**.
> • **SW wind / Föhn:** Clears the valley fast.
>
> Live Bise detection updates automatically at **abovethefog.ch**.
